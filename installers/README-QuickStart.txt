QUICK INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "VocalRemover-Setup.exe".
2. Click "Install & Launch".
3. The software will install to your computer and create a shortcut 
   on your Desktop and Start Menu with the microphone icon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURES & HIGHLIGHTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 100% Private & Device-Local: Your audio files are never uploaded to
  any remote cloud server. Processing stays on your PC.
* Zero Vocal Bleed: Neural AI separation calibrated to remove stereo 
  reverb and backing delays while protecting kick drums, bass, and flutes.
* Multitrack Player: Play Instrumental Solo, Vocals Solo, or Preview Mix.
* YouTube Link Support: Paste any YouTube link to fetch and separate.
* DAW-Ready Export: Save stems in 16-bit PCM stereo WAV or 320kbps MP3.
* 100% Offline: Operates without requiring an active internet connection.
